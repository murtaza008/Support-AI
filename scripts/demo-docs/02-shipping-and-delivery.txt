Shipping and Delivery

Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days within the United
States and is free on orders over $75.

Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days and costs $18 flat.

International shipping is available to 24 countries and takes
7 to 14 business days. Customs fees are the customer's responsibility.

Tracking numbers are emailed within 24 hours of dispatch. Orders
placed after 2pm EST ship the following business day.

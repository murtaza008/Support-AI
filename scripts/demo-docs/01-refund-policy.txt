Refund Policy

Customers can request a full refund within 30 days of purchase by
emailing support@nimbusrobotics.com with their order number.

Refunds are processed within 5 business days of approval and are
returned to the original payment method.

Orders placed with a promotional discount of more than 50% are
eligible for store credit only, not a cash refund.

Our support hours are Monday to Friday, 9am to 6pm EST.

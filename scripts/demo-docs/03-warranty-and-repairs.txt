Warranty and Repairs

All Nimbus hardware comes with a 24-month limited warranty covering
manufacturing defects.

The warranty does not cover water damage, drops, or damage caused by
unauthorised repairs.

To start a warranty claim, email support@nimbusrobotics.com with your
serial number and a photo of the issue. Approved repairs are completed
within 10 business days.

Out-of-warranty repairs are charged at $95 plus parts.

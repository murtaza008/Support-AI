Subscription and Billing

Plans are billed monthly on the date you subscribed. You can switch
plans at any time and the change takes effect immediately, with
charges prorated.

Cancelling stops the next renewal but does not refund the current month.

Annual plans are billed once per year and include two months free.
Annual plans can be cancelled within 14 days of purchase for a
full refund.

Invoices are emailed automatically and are available under Settings,
then Billing.
